# Citing SCAM and further reading

SCAM is verified against the PATO reference solver's `AblationTestCase`
benchmark suite. If you use SCAM, the following describe the reference
solver and the TACOT benchmark material it is verified against:

- Lachaud, J. & Mansour, N.N. (2014). *Porous-material Analysis Toolbox
  Based on OpenFOAM and Applications.* Journal of Thermophysics and Heat
  Transfer, 28(2).
- Lachaud, J. et al. (2014). *Validation of a volume-averaged fiber-scale
  model for the thermal and chemical response of carbon-fiber preforms.*
  International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer.
- *TACOT: Theoretical Ablative Composite for Open Testing.* Ablation
  Workshop 2011.

See [`01_licensing_and_data.md`](01_licensing_and_data.md) for the
licensing terms of PATO itself and of the third-party reference datasets
bundled in this repository.
